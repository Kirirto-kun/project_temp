# Jury questions

- **Where does the ceiling come from?** From the range you entered; it is a scenario setting. We say “against your range”, never “your boss would have paid”.
- **Why not let an AI negotiate for me?** A bill can be delegated; a conversation with your manager or landlord is yours to say.
- **Why come back?** Several money conversations a year, a dated plan, free replays, new scenarios.
- **What is hard-coded?** No numbers: amounts come from the parser and the engine; the repository is open.
- **How accurate are the voice metrics?** Pace, pauses, fillers, loudness and pitch are measurements with stated limits; tension is an index with a visible formula; persuasion is labelled as a model assessment.
- **Why no video avatar?** Out of scope for this build; the coach is clearly an AI with a flat mark, which also keeps the data boundary simple: no camera is used.
