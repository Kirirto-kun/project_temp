Holdline logo options

Three marks, each shown at 24, 32 and 64px, in brand colour and in one-colour (monochrome) form, on light and dark. Wordmark: the site's display sans (Geist, bold, tight tracking), never monospace. Option A is the one implemented in src/components/holdline/Logo.tsx and src/app/icon.svg.

A. Crossline

Implementedoption-a-crossline.svg

Two posts, the two sides of the table, and one line that runs through both and past them. It reads as an H for Holdline and as a line held level across the gap. Pure rectangles, so it stays crisp at 16 to 20px and works as a favicon.
